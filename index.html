Chart 7: Colorado Wolf Depredations by Livestock

Coloradoans voted for wolves in 2020, but unless the compensation fund grows with livestock losses, reintroduction should be paused for reevaluation.

Livestock losses rose from 29 animals in 2024 to 67 so far in 2026, so compensation funding must grow or reintroduction should pause.

Chart 8: Colorado Depredation Compensation Budget

CPW paid or approved about $753,000 for 2025–26 wolf losses, more than double the $350,000 fund. Without more funding, reintroduction cannot fairly continue.
